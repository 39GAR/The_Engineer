About Me

Hi, I’m Edgar Sebuti Maseepe Mamogawane but online I go by SMME. The name comes from my initials, but it also plays on SME (Small & Medium Enterprises), which connects me naturally with people and brands I enjoy working with.

I’ve been exploring web development for a while and recently launched my first live website, a milestone that proves I’m not just learning, but building. I call myself new in the making but old in the learning, every project I take adds another trick up my sleeve.

I’m currently studying Aeronautical Engineering at the University of the Witwatersrand. I chose this field because I thrive on challenges and want to contribute to sustainable engineering. My focus is on the body design of mobile machines, making sure that they are as aerodynamically efficient as possible, helping reduce fuel consumption while enabling lighter, less powerful electric motors to be truly functional, a step towards balancing innovation with environmental care.

Beyond engineering and code, I’m deeply drawn to creativity and art from fashion and painting to sculpture, barbering, and anything where imagination comes to life through hands-on creation. That creative energy flows into the way I design websites, clean, bold, and built to tell a story.

In short: I’m a creator, a problem-solver, and a student of both technology and art working to build experiences that feel alive, purposeful, and sustainable.
