About Me

Hi, my name is Edgar Maseepe Mamogawane, also known as Sebuti. “Sebuti” is a childhood name that I still use because it fits perfectly with the identity I’m building today: SMME — pronounced sme with a soft, lower “e,” kind of like how Kanye says his name. “SMME” carries a few meanings. One of them is Small, Medium and Micro Enterprises, which is the business category I’m part of and the space I aim to grow and collaborate within. But more personally, SMME is an abbreviation of my full name: Sebuti Maseepe Mamogawane Edgar. I created it back in 2020–2021 when I was planning to become a DJ and wanted a name that sounded mysterious and coded — almost like a secret service agent. “S double M E.” :D.

I’m someone who has recently developed a strong interest in software and web development. Over the past year or two I’ve completed courses in HTML, CSS, Java, Python, and MATLAB, and since then I’ve been consistently pushing myself to get better, faster, and more accurate with my code. My main goal is to offer these skills to local businesses helping them build the websites and systems they need to grow their craft. I’ve noticed that many new businesses need an online presence, yet a lot of local entrepreneurs don’t have the guidance to get started. Because of that, I taught myself how to set up domains, build static sites, and create booking systems to help fill that gap. That’s the level I’m at right now, but I’m someone who always learns beyond what I’m currently doing, so I’m confident I’ll have even more to bring to the table as I keep growing.
