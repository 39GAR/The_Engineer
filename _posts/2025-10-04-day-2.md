So it's been sometime now and I have been reaching out to certain brands,
obviously I had favorites but seems like I am not the favorites' favorite.
The week has been hard, I am starting to get what it means to reach out and
how hard that actually is. But there's a lot of progress I am getting better,
faster and more professional day by day. I am less emotional about it now.

But one good thing is I found the archive to my old art images and the beats
I used to produce while growing up. They are lovely and build momentum to why
keep going, because of my love for art. I believe my one of my biggest reasons
behind web development was to help local artists to have an online platform
which will professionally display their work.

So one of the images I displayed above and is also one you may find on my
insta account ""@S.M.M.E"". I didn't have it for a while and I remembered that
I actually used my home laptop to generate them and thank God they were still
there. It is currently 01:30, there was a party at my res, I think it's over
now bcoz I hear people leaving. Unfortunately I couldn't attend, can't say I
wanted to but showing face sometimes is good for business.

So the story behind this image by the way, it's similar to the one I posted
last time. So I believe I made this in 2023, somewhere in the time frame
from September to November. So at that time I was trying to establish a
brand of my own, the Idea was to build hype through cool characters. So
I would make a character have it wear the coming collection and see people's
response and if a certain set gets the most positive reviews then it would
go to the next stage which is prototyping, display and hopefully release.

This is me, SMME signing out for today.
Bless.
